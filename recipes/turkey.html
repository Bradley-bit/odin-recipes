Turkey

Description

This slow cooker turkey breast recipe always comes out juicy, tender, and flavorful every time — and it only takes two ingredients. The hardest part is waiting for it to finish cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 (6 pound) bone-in turkey breast
  • 1 (1 ounce) envelope dry onion soup mix

Steps

  1. Pat turkey dry with paper towels. Remove excess skin, leaving skin on breast.
  2. Rub soup mix over outside and under skin of turkey.
  3. Place in a slow cooker. Cover, and cook on High for 1 hour; set to Low, and cook for 7 hours. An instant-read thermometer inserted near the bone should read 165 degrees F (74 degrees C).
  4. Serve with gravy.
